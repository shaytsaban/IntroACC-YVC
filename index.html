Course Aims

Unlock the ability to provide useful financial information for sound economic decisions. By the end, you'll master:

  • Core Principles: Conceptual frameworks of accounting.

  • Financial Statements: Balance Sheet, P&L, Equity Changes.

About the Lecturer

He brings extensive field experience, having served on the Examination Committee of the Board of Accountants at the Ministry of Justice, ensuring you learn not just theory, but practice.

Lecture 1: Basics of Financial Accounting Values

The five fundamental elements of accounting. The first step to reading financial statements.

  • Assets: Economic resources (Cash, Machinery).
  • Liabilities: Obligations to settle in the future.
  • Equity: Residual interest (Assets - Liabilities).

Lecture 7: Fixed Assets & Depreciation

Introduction to Property, Plant, and Equipment (PP&E). Key concepts include cost measurement, depreciation methods (Straight-Line, Sum-of-Years), and disposal.

Key Concepts

  • • Definition: Tangible assets, >1 year life.
  • • Cost: Purchase + installation costs.
  • • Depreciation: Allocation of cost over life.

Key Formulas

CV = Cost - Accord. Dep.

Straight-Line = (Cost - Residual) / Life

Sum-Digits = n(n + 1) / 2
